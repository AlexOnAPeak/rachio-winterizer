Rachio Winterizer

Blow out your sprinkler lines with an air compressor while your Rachio controller cycles through the zones. A wait between zones gives the compressor time to build pressure back up.

This page runs entirely in your browser. It talks only to api.rach.io, and a Content Security Policy blocks it from contacting any other server.

🧪 Demo mode. Fake controllers, nothing is sent to Rachio, and time runs 60× faster.

Getting your Rachio API key

  1. Log in at app.rach.io
  2. Open your profile / account settings
  3. Click Get API Key and copy it

This key gives full control of your Rachio account. Only paste it into a copy of this page that you saved yourself and have read.
